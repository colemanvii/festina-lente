Log Entry

All we ever have is now.

Most of life’s work is learning to live inside it—unburdened by the ghosts of past and future selves. To be present without flinching. To inhabit the moment without reaching for the next one.

Writing has always been my way back into that quiet center. A way of slowing down the mind until the world comes back into focus. When I write, time loosens its grip. The noise fades. What’s left is a kind of stillness I can hear.

Painting opens that same door, just differently. With color, breath, and repetition, it pulls me into the same slow realm—what I’ve started calling the long now. A place beneath urgency. A place where attention feels whole again.

Guard the signal. Move deliberately. Build what lasts.
